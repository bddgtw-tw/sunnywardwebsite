will not rust. When exposed to moisture, fibre glass insulation neither
absorbs not holds water. It is its low weight-to-strength ratio. As a rule of
thumb, for the same strength, fibreglass will weight approximately one
seventh as much as steel, and half as much as aluminium.

is used for many products such as aircraft, boats, automobiles, bath tubs
and swimming pools, tanks, roofing, pipes and so on.

About Us
With various styles, finishes, and colors, this collection allows
designers to create customized outdoor environments.
Whether for a luxury retreat or a cozy backyard, our expertly

The Balau Wood Collection by Sunnyward Sdn Bhd offers
timeless, durable outdoor furniture crafted from Balau wood
(Bangkirai). Designed to withstand humidity, rain, and
extreme weather, it combines strength, weather resistance,
and aesthetic appeal, making it ideal for gardens, patios,
resorts, and commercial spaces.
--- Page 3 ---
Balau wood, also known as Bangkirai, is a dense and durable hardwood from the
Shorea genus, found in Southeast Asia. Valued for its strength, decay resistance,
and natural oils, it is a top choice for outdoor furniture, decking, and
construction. Comparable to Teak and Ipe, Balau offers similar durability at a
more cost-effective price, making it ideal for high-quality, long-lasting outdoor
projects.
With its rich grain and warm tones, Balau wood enhances landscape and
architectural designs. Naturally resilient to humidity, pests, and harsh weather, it
requires minimal maintenance, though occasional oiling or sealing helps preserve
its beauty. Its strength and aesthetic appeal make it the perfect material for
premium outdoor furniture that lasts for years.
